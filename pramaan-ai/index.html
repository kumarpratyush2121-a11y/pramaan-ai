🛡️ Forensic Rulebook & Authenticity Factors Matrix

Scientific and mathematical breakdown of all factors checked to distinguish authentic vs. fake documents.
🔬

1. Error Level Analysis (ELA)

When an image is saved as a JPEG, each 8×8 macroblock is quantized at a uniform error level. If a section (like a Date of Birth or photo) is spliced or edited in Photoshop, that region undergoes a different number of re-compression cycles, causing a high-frequency error gradient spike clearly visible on our thermal heatmap overlay.

🧮

2. Verhoeff Dihedral Checksum ($D_5$)

Aadhaar numbers use the Verhoeff algorithm based on the dihedral group of order 10 ($D_5$). It catches 100% of single-digit transcription errors and 95.3% of adjacent transposition errors. Amateur fake UID generators almost always fail this mathematical check.

👁️

3. Deep ArcFace Biometric Embeddings

Computes normalized 128D/512D deep facial feature vectors from the ID portrait and reference selfie/webcam using Cosine Distance $\cos(\theta) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\|\|\mathbf{v}\|}$. Gracefully handles lighting variations while catching deepfakes and photo-substitution impersonators.

💳

4. PAN 4th Character Tax Entity Rules

Income Tax Department PAN cards follow strict semantic encoding: the 4th character denotes entity status (P = Individual, C = Company, H = HUF, F = Firm, T = Trust), and the 5th character must match the first letter of the holder's last name.

🌐

5. Passport MRZ ICAO 9303 Check-Digits

2-line Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) encoding uses a weighted modulo-10 matrix with weighting sequence $[7, 3, 1, 7, 3, 1 \dots]$ over document number, date of birth, and expiry date fields to prevent passport forgery.

🛡️

6. Guilloché Security Patterns & Microprint

Authentic IDs feature high-frequency sine-wave Guilloché background patterns that degrade and blur when copied or scanned by flatbed scanners, revealing non-genuine document reproduction.
